# Nevada County population and home values: model results

All prices are real 2025 dollars (CPI-U deflated). Generated by `model.py`.

## Stage 1: population and real home-price growth, peer counties

Dependent variable: annual log change in each county's real FHFA house price index.
Year fixed effects absorb statewide and national drivers such as mortgage rates.

Headline set: the 30 California counties with mean population under 200,000, the set that includes Nevada County (102,000).
27 of them have price data.
Large counties show a much larger association (see specification curve).

| Term | Coefficient | 90% CI | p-value |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Last year's real price growth | 0.235 | 0.056 to 0.414 | 0.031 |
| Population growth (log change) | 0.817 | 0.300 to 1.334 | 0.009 |
| Real per-capita income growth | -0.071 | -0.179 to 0.037 | 0.278 |
| Last year's permits per 1,000 residents | -0.001 | -0.003 to 0.000 | 0.181 |

27 counties, years 2001-2024, n = 648. County and year fixed effects. Standard errors clustered by county and year.

**Long-run elasticity of price to population, momentum model: 1.07** (90% interval 0.44 to 1.69).
A county population that is 1% larger, sustained, goes with real home prices about 1.1% higher
once price momentum plays out.

## Placebo check

These fits use all counties.

Next year's population growth, which this year's prices cannot respond to, is included as a
regressor. If it predicts this year's price growth, the population coefficient reflects shared
local shocks (jobs, amenities, remote work) rather than a timing story.

Next year's population growth on its own:

| Term | Coefficient | 90% CI | p-value |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Last year's real price growth | 0.376 | 0.229 to 0.522 | 0.000 |
| Next year's population growth (placebo) | 0.928 | 0.540 to 1.316 | 0.000 |
| Real per-capita income growth | -0.035 | -0.141 to 0.072 | 0.590 |
| Last year's permits per 1,000 residents | -0.001 | -0.002 to 0.001 | 0.437 |

55 counties, years 2001-2024, n = 1320. County and year fixed effects. Standard errors clustered by county and year.

## Population scenarios, 2026-2035

Each scenario holds Nevada County population growth at a constant rate. Each year's growth is
combined with the local-projection response at every later horizon. Every other driver stays
where it was in 2025, so the 2035 value is the 2025 value plus the population effect only.
It is not a forecast of the market. Ranges come from 1,000 county bootstrap resamples of the peer-county local projections.
